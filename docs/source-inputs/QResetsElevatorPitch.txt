It's a project called QuickResets. A QR code and a single dollar.

You spot a code on a bus stop or a bulletin board. You scan it, give one buck, and that money moves straight to someone in a critical situation. Just a clean line from you to them.

Early on, a little of that might need to cover the secure payment systems just to keep things running right. The ultimate plan is to have company revenue pay for all that overhead so every single cent goes to the need. We just want to use micro-donations to actually change things in the community. You start by helping one person.

This is for people whose world just collapsed. Maybe false accusations ruined their reputation overnight. A parent out of groceries. Someone staring down their first night on the street after a string of bad luck. Capable people drowning quietly because they don't know how to ask for help or they think they'll be judged. We aren't just talking about hypotheticals here.

Cash matters right away. But they need more than that. We want to connect them with advocates who can bridge the gap to the right agencies. When life hits you that hard, you don't need a stack of forms to fill out alone. Capable people can spiral from bad to worse in total silence without real support. The reset needs to be a real path forward.

We're in the building phase now. Working on the payment rail and the landing page so every dollar moves without a detour. Then we'll print the first codes and get them up around Phoenix Metro, Sedona, wherever they make sense. We also need to build up the quiet outreach that actually finds people before they fall deeper.

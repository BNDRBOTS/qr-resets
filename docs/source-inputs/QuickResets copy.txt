QuickResets (QR) is a direct-aid system for people who have experienced a total life collapse in a very short amount of time. It is specifically for those who had a support system—family, a career, or a reputation—suddenly taken from them by false allegations, accidents, or other life-altering events. We focus on people facing "impending doom" who usually suffer in silence and don't know how to ask for help.
The system works by placing small QR codes cards in public areas where people can scan and give a dollar. That money goes strictly toward a "Reset"—helping someone in a critical spot. Beyond immediate cash, the goal is to provide advocates who can help people get set up with the right agencies that meet their specific needs.

**Who QuickResets is for:**
 * **The Support-System Loss:** People who had a family or network that was suddenly removed due to an accident or rift.
 * **The Falsely Accused:** People whose reputations and lives were dismantled by false allegations over a short window.
 * **The Rapid Collapse:** Anyone who experienced significant life events in a very short timeframe and is now facing impending doom.
 * **The Silent Sufferers:** People who don't typically tell anyone they are struggling and need someone to find them and let them know help is there.
**The Mission Forward:**
We aren't just giving out cash; we are building a network of advocates to bridge the gap between a total loss and a new start. We find the people who are doing it in silence, provide the critical help they need to stop the downward spiral, and connect them with the agencies that can help them rebuild.
